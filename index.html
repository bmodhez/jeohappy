🎂 Our Story

Born from a love
of celebration.

🎂
Rated4.8 ★

Born from a love for celebration and the art of baking — Jeohappyday began as a dream to make every day feel special in Virar. Nestled in Sneha Nagar CHS, our cozy cake shop has become Virar's go-to destination for freshly baked cakes, artisan pastries, and quality coffee.

We believe every occasion — big or small — deserves something extraordinary. Proudly women-owned and LGBTQ+ friendly, where everyone is welcome.
